1 Our story

Built around the work,
not the website.

Pier 60 USA was founded in Dallas as a general maintenance shop serving a handful of restaurant operators who were tired of coordinating four trades for every job. What started as a convenience became a model — we kept the crews in-house, kept the scopes honest, and kept showing up when the calls came in at odd hours.

Today the company covers everything from preventative HVAC programs to full-scope renovations, with a roster of W-2 technicians, in-house electricians and plumbers, and a project management team that runs scope, schedule, and budget on a single shared doc.

We're not the biggest facility services company in Texas. We aim to be the easiest one to work with — the one your portfolio manager forwards to peers when they ask "who do you use?"

2 How we work

Four principles,
repeated until they're boring.

01 / Principle

Show up on time. Every time.

A schedule is a promise. We treat every appointment — emergency or routine — like it's the only one that day. If we're going to be late, you hear it from us before the slot, not after.

02 / Principle

Quote tight. Document everything.

Our quotes don't include padding for "in case." If a scope changes mid-job, you get a change order in writing before any work happens. Photos, line-items, and sign-offs at every stage.

03 / Principle

In-house, always.

We don't subcontract the work we lead with. Our HVAC, electrical, plumbing, and renovation teams are W-2 — trained together, accountable to one PM, and consistent across every site.

04 / Principle

One call. One vendor. One invoice.

The reason our customers consolidate work with us isn't price — it's the operational savings of routing every facility need through a single channel. We make it look simple on purpose.
